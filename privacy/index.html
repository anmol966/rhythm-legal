Privacy Policy

Rhythm – Video Journal · Effective 6 November 2026

In short: your journal stays on your phone. Rhythm has no accounts and no servers. It contains no ads, analytics or tracking. We never receive, see or sell your entries.

Who we are

Rhythm – Video Journal ("Rhythm", "the app") is developed by fennec. "We" and "us" in this policy mean the developer. You can reach us at anmolpanwarr966@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your phone

Everything you put in your journal is saved in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Videos and photos you record or add, and the text you write.
  • The date and time of each entry.
  • If you allow location, an approximate place name (such as a neighbourhood and city) and approximate coordinates for each entry.
  • If you turn on the app lock, your PIN and your security-question answer. These are stored only as one-way scrambled values (salted hashes) in your phone's secure system storage, never as the PIN or answer itself.
  • Your app settings, such as your weekly goal and theme.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. We have no way to see, access or recover it.

What leaves your phone

Place names. When you create an entry with location allowed, the app gets your approximate location from your phone's location service. It then asks your phone's built-in geocoding service to turn the coordinates into a place name. This service is provided by Google on Android and by Apple on iOS. To do this, your phone sends the approximate coordinates to Google or Apple. That use is covered by Google's privacy policy or Apple's privacy policy. If you turn location off, or remove the place from an entry, nothing is sent.

Saving to your gallery. If you choose to save a video or photo from an entry to your phone's gallery, a copy is placed there. It is then handled like any other photo on your phone.

Device backups. On Android, Rhythm turns off system backup, so your journal is not copied to Google's backups. On iOS, the app's data is included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) if you have device backup turned on. Apple handles that backup.

Nothing else leaves your phone.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No servers: we do not collect, store or process your data anywhere.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • No selling, sharing or transferring of your data to anyone.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera and microphone: to record video entries. Used only while you are recording.
  • Approximate location (optional): to label entries with a place name. Used only while the app is open, never in the background. The app works fully without it.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock (optional): to unlock the app. Your phone checks your fingerprint or face itself. The app only learns whether the check passed and never receives biometric data.
  • Photos and videos: the app uses your phone's system picker. It can only see the items you choose to add, and it saves its own copy of them.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your entries stay on your phone until you delete them. A deleted entry goes to "Recently Deleted" for 30 days so you can restore it. After that, it is removed permanently. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone.

Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on request. Your journal is gone if you uninstall the app or lose your phone, unless it is included in an iPhone device backup as described above.

Children

Rhythm is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The app collects no personal information at all.

Future features

The app mentions planned AI features as "coming soon". None are active today. If a future version needs to send any of your data off your phone, we will update this policy first and ask for your permission in the app before doing so.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
